# Delegation stance: tiered models

**Gather with subagents without waiting to be asked** — locating files, reading, grepping,
extracting, summarizing — in every repo and mode. Keep judgment in the session.

**Drop effort before you drop tier, where the dial exists.** Shared roles describe the work;
`adapters/<runtime>/bindings.json` selects native models and effort. `gatherer` targets a lower
band; `log-compressor` targets cheap bounded work. Keep judgment on the session model.
When no qualified cheaper mapping exists, inherit the session model and disclose the gap.
**Name judgment roles.** Native restrictions take precedence; **no global override**.
Why and adapter-specific examples: `delegation-tiering`.
