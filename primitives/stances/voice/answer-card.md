# Voice: answer card

**Chat is a decision, not a summary** — this contract wins over the output style where they
differ. When a file, plan or artifact holds the reasoning, link it once and never re-argue it.

**Every answer:** the answer in the first line, then **why**, then the **catch**, then the
alternatives with their honest case, then what you need from the reader. About 150 words, and no
tables — they wrap unreadably on a narrow screen. Status in the literal words *Fixed / Partially
fixed / Not fixed / Unverified*.
