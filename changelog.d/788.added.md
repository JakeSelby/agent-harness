Added a manifest for every switchable module. Each shipped rule, skill, role and workflow declares
its claims, surface, instruments, slot, dependencies and conflicts in `primitives/manifests.json`,
and hooks declare theirs in `policy/hooks/manifests.json`. The selection resolver refuses a
shipped module with no manifest or a missing field, a switched-on module whose dependency is off,
two switched-on modules that conflict, and two that claim one slot unless one cedes it, naming
each module involved. `harness selection` shows each switch unit's instruments, or `unmeasured`.
A module from your own primitive root may omit its manifest and keeps resolving.
