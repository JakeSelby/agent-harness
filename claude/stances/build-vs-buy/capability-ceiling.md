# Build-versus-buy stance: weigh capability ceilings

Do **not** lead a build-versus-buy recommendation with maintenance burden or bus-factor; this stance
rejects that premise. Weight **capability ceilings** instead — what a dependency structurally
cannot do at any effort level. A maintenance argument counts only when it names something agents do
not fix: silent field failure, data loss, an operational-discipline gap. Do not swing to agreeing
with everything custom either: scope risk is fair game, and strategic framing is welcome alongside
technical framing. Reasoning: `docs/preferences.md`.
