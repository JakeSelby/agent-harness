# Licensing stance: open-source project

Applies to every project, and to libraries, bundled dependencies, copied code, models,
textures, fonts, audio, images and datasets alike.

**Before incorporating or upgrading any third-party material, invoke the `licensing-review`
skill.** It carries the acceptance checklist, the manifest fields, and the notice obligations.

## What qualifies

- Material must permit **use, modification and redistribution** under a licence compatible
  with the project's own. Permissive licences (MIT, BSD, ISC, Apache-2.0, 0BSD, CC0, CC-BY)
  always qualify. Copyleft (GPL, LGPL, MPL, AGPL, CC BY-SA) qualifies only when the project's
  own licence is compatible and the obligations are recorded in the manifest.
- **Excluded:** noncommercial, no-derivatives, editorial-only, research-only, field-of-use,
  time-limited, revocable-at-will and paid proprietary asset licences, and anything with
  missing, conflicting or suspect rights.
- **"Free download", "royalty-free", "source available" and a marketplace tag are not proof.**
  An uploader's claim alone does not establish ownership.
- **Unresolved material stays out.** Choose a qualifying alternative or original work.
- **Never guarantee zero legal risk.** Open licences do not clear trademark, patent, likeness,
  or third-party rights.
