# Licensing stance: permissive, commercial-safe

Applies to every project, and to libraries, bundled dependencies, copied code, models,
textures, fonts, audio, images and datasets alike.

**Before incorporating or upgrading any third-party material, invoke the `licensing-review`
skill.** It carries the acceptance checklist, the manifest fields, and the notice obligations.
This instruction is the trigger — do not wait to recognize a licensing question on your own,
because the case this policy exists to catch is the one that does not look like one.

## Hard prohibitions, always in force

- Use only material permitting **commercial use, modification, and redistribution in a
  closed-source paid product**, with no acquisition fees, royalties, subscriptions, revenue
  caps, or future payment requirement for the version used.
- **Preferred software licenses:** MIT, BSD-2-Clause, BSD-3-Clause, ISC, Apache-2.0, 0BSD.
  **Preferred content licenses:** CC0-1.0, CC-BY-4.0. These are candidates, not a substitute
  for checking the exact version and any bundled material.
- **Excluded, without exception:** copyleft and share-alike — GPL, AGPL, LGPL, MPL, CC BY-SA
  and ODbL. Also noncommercial, no-derivatives, editorial-only, research-only, field-of-use,
  advertising-credit, time-limited, revocable-at-will, and paid proprietary asset licenses.
- **"Free download", "royalty-free", "source available" and a marketplace tag are not proof.**
  An uploader's license claim alone does not establish ownership. No game rips, no unlicensed
  copies, no material with missing, conflicting or suspect rights.
- **Unresolved material stays out of production.** Choose a qualifying alternative or original
  work. Do not silently waive this policy.
- **Never guarantee zero legal risk.** Open licenses do not clear trademark, patent, likeness,
  or third-party rights.

Standalone development tools may use copyleft licenses where merely using them imposes no
obligation on your output. Check bundled runtime components separately. This exception never
permits incorporating copyleft code or assets.
