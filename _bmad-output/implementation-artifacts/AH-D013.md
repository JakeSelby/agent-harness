# AH-D013 — Align the PRD, architecture spine and milestone map to the measurable-harness roadmap

This work item was authored as part of the repository's committed BMad planning system.

## Context

The layered-harness vision (#778, AH-D012) made the MVP claim concrete: every module switchable, attributable and measured, with a defensible result against bare Claude Code. PRD §9 still ordered the milestones for an earlier plan. It put the `superpowers` mode and the four-arm benchmark in v0.14.0, and tied a published live cost result to decision-provider consumers in v0.15.0. The 2026-09-24 review of the release cost evaluation then found that the replay's bar cannot support a claim. Pass and fail were not saved for the eight-task runs, no subagent spawned in any of the 64 saved runs, and the two arms had different web access. The question is how to reorder the corpus so the claim ships first, and what evidence standard SM-2 must meet.

## Options

- **Keep §9.2 and fix the replay inside v0.14.0.** Least churn, but the MVP still waits on the Superpowers mode and on Jev consumers, and SM-2's three-rep ratio stays too weak to defend.
- **A single "Measured" v0.14.0.** This was the first round of the roadmap review. It moves the measurement work forward, but it leaves no room for the control plane that makes each module switchable and attributable.
- **Five milestones ordered by what the claim cannot ship without.** v0.14.0 Switchable, v0.15.0 Measured (the MVP), v0.16.0 Composable, v0.17.0 Real work and v1.0.0 Stable. Interop, randomised sessions and Codex parity follow the MVP. It moves every open issue, and the Superpowers north star slips two milestones.

## Decision

Five milestones, recorded as a hybrid of direct adjustment and an MVP review, with major scope. The maintainer's roadmap review of 2026-09-24 approved it with all decisions accepted:

- The Jev judge lands in v0.16.0, and #135 and the policy preferences park in the backlog.
- The bench is decoupled from the `superpowers` mode, and the #553 coexistence spike runs in v0.15.0.
- Randomised sessions and the external task set move to v0.17.0.
- SM-2 headlines Cost-of-Pass and pass rate, with task-clustered paired 95% intervals and five or more trials per task and arm. The task set is sized by a power analysis, with the minimum detectable effect stated before the run. The 0.85 bar becomes a pre-registered hypothesis. It is supported only when the paired, task-clustered interval on the Cost-of-Pass ratio excludes 1.0 with the point estimate at or below 0.85, and pass-rate non-inferiority holds against a pre-registered margin of one task's share of the set.
- The v1.0.0 contract gains an evaluation gate: a candidate ships only with a verified proof bundle.
- The spine records the module manifest (AD-22), zero-footprint observation with per-module attribution (AD-23), and estimand labels as an amendment to AD-12.

## Consequences

- **Easier:** each milestone has one exit test, and the claim no longer waits on interop or on Jev consumers. A null or negative result is still publishable, because the hypothesis and the decision rule are fixed first.
- **Harder:** v0.14.0 carries about 37 existing issues and 7 new ones. Five or more trials per task and arm raise the cost of each live run. If delegation never fires (#429), proof set 1 reports the cost claim as unsupported.
- **Must follow:**
  - step 3 files the twenty new stories, maps the proposal's working labels to their BMad IDs and issues in an appended amendment, and moves every open issue to its milestone;
  - the evaluation-gate story updates the v1 release plan;
  - status lines that cite a closed issue were re-derived here from tag containment; other stale "unreleased" lines, which predate 0.13.0, are a follow-up chore.
