## Story

As a developer selecting stances, I want the stance conflict engine to ship real constraints, so that a contradictory selection is reported, and never projected, instead of the engine sitting empty.

## Context and value

The conflict engine's `constraints.json` supported `when`, `requires`, `excludes` and `reason` but shipped empty, while the repository already held a contradiction it would catch: `delegation: tiered` says "Never `frontier`", yet the `designer` and `design-judge` roles declare `tier: frontier` ([#464](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/464)). PR #473 adds that the field scan listed the empty engine as an honest gap.

The issue carries the v0.13.0 milestone and names #116 as its parent. It was closed as completed on 2026-09-22 by PR #473.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** `delegation: tiered`, **when** a role declares `tier: frontier`, **then** a constraint reports the contradiction unless the role is one the skill-level exception names; `designer` and `design-judge` are the fixture.
2. **Given** the nine stance dimensions, **when** the constraint file is read, **then** it carries at least two further constraints, each with a `reason`.
3. **Given** a violated constraint, **when** `harness stances --json` or `harness lint` runs, **then** each reports it.

## Design

### Approach

As built in PR #473:
- `primitives/constraints.json` ships four constraints, each with a reason:
  - `delegation: tiered` excludes roles declaring `tier: frontier`, except `designer` and `design-judge`;
  - `cost: max` excludes `delegation: off`;
  - `commits: as-you-go` excludes `autonomy: confirm-writes`;
  - `commits: as-you-go` excludes `autonomy: ask`.
- The schema gains `excludes_roles`, matched against the front matter of `primitives/roles/*.md`, makes `reason` required and rejects unknown fields. The schema is documented in `docs/primitive-authoring.md`.
- `harness stances --json` reports `conflicts` with their reasons, `harness lint` reports them as findings, and the resolver keeps them as hard errors, so sync never projects a contradiction.

[Source: primitives/constraints.json] [PR #473](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/pull/473)

### Decisions and alternatives

- The design roles are exempted by name in the constraint's `allow` list, mirroring the exemption the `delegation-tiering` skill states [Source: primitives/constraints.json].
- No rejected alternative is recorded in the issue or pull request.
