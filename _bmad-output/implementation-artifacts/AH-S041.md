## Story

As a developer who wants review sized to the change, I want review depth and review independence to be separate selectable policies, so that the review workflow runs what I chose and says plainly when the independence I asked for was not achieved.

## Context and value

The review workflow fixes two sequential fresh-context reviewers, and the core rules mix model-family and context independence ([#124](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/124)). On `main`, `/review` still spawns `spec-reviewer` and then `reviewer`, each in its own fresh context [Source: primitives/workflows/review.md], and `primitives/rules/delegation.md` still says "never verify with the same family and shared context".

The epic's 2026-09-21 assessment rated the story Not delivered: no `review/` dimension exists outside the plan ([#116 comment](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/116)). That still holds under `primitives/stances/`.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** a selected review depth, **when** `/review` dispatches, **then** it follows that depth, and under `delegation=off` it does not silently spawn a reviewer.
2. **Given** a selected independence, **when** a review runs, **then** the reported independence describes what actually executed, and an unsupported requirement yields an explicit unresolved condition.
3. **Given** every depth variant, **when** tested with missing model families, same-context attempts and `off` interactions, **then** same-context review is rejected as independent and findings are validated deterministically.

## Design

### Approach

The issue proposes `review` with `self-check`, `independent`, `scope-and-quality` and `risk-adaptive`, and `review-independence` with `fresh-context` and `different-family`. The default is `scope-and-quality` with `fresh-context`; a different family is requested when available and selected. Unachievable independence is reported, and self-review is never relabelled as independent ([#124](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/124)).

The plan fixes risk-adaptive selection criteria before implementation: presentation-only edits may self-check, logic changes need independent review, and authorization, secrets, persistence and migration changes need separate scope and quality review. A missing capability cannot silently downgrade the required review [Source: docs/plans/stance-completeness.md#Step 4 — New choices].

### Decisions and alternatives

- The explicit `fresh-context` default resolves the old rule that combined family and context; the plan records it as a policy clarification in migration notes, not as byte-identical behaviour [Source: docs/plans/stance-completeness.md#Step 4 — New choices].
- With delegation off, the workflow reports independent review as unperformed, or self-checks only where the selected policy allows [Source: docs/plans/stance-completeness.md#Step 4 — New choices].

### Do not implement

- Do not relabel self-review as independent.
- Do not launch a reviewer under `delegation=off`.
- Do not sync an unmerged candidate into the live installation.
