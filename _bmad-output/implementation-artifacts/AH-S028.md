## Story

As a developer who already runs Claude Code, Codex or both, I want installation, sync and uninstall to own
only what the harness writes, record what it changed, and survive interruption and concurrent runs, so
that my own configuration is never corrupted and removing the harness restores what I had.

## Context and value

A work package of epic #93 that depends on #94. The issue records the v0.8.0 defects that motivated it: an
unsupported Codex untrusted approval mode was emitted, same-name nested TOML keys were rewritten, clean
Codex homes were skipped, generated-file drift was missed, and owned settings were left behind after
uninstall. [Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/95]

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** each runtime alone and both together, **when** clean install, upgrade, repeat sync, custom
   homes, malformed configuration, interruption, concurrent sync, drift, user-edit conflicts and uninstall
   are exercised, **then** each passes.
2. **Given** unrelated bytes and settings, **when** any of those operations runs, **then** they survive
   unchanged.

Taken from the issue's acceptance section. Adoption of divergent skills and personal text through
preserved backups and visible conflicts is part of the issue's proposed design, not its acceptance.

## Design

### Approach

Independent runtime targets and documented custom homes; structural, style-preserving TOML editing;
ownership records holding prior and applied values; atomic writes and locks. Divergent skills and personal
text are adopted through preserved backups and visible conflicts. Identity inputs are shared, and JSONC is
inspected deliberately. Any vendored parser is reviewed and pinned with shipped notices.
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/95]

Delivered through two slices:
- AH-S068 / #164 (PR #102): independent bootstrap, structural TOML and JSON editing, the ownership ledger,
  and the vendored TOMLKit 0.15.1 with notices.
- AH-S074 / #170 (PR #108): intent recorded before mutation and recovery of redirected links and backups.

### Decisions and alternatives

- **Structural, style-preserving TOML editing** over line-based rewriting.
- **Record prior and applied values** so uninstall restores only what still matches.
- **Vendor a reviewed, pinned parser** with notices rather than an unpinned dependency.

### Do not implement

- A Cursor or Grok adapter, hosted-agent support, a native memory merge or UML viewer work.
