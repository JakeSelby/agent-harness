## Story

As a developer comparing tools, I want a dated, sourced reading of the field that says when to use
something else, so that I can trust the repository's uniqueness claim because its own gaps are listed
first.

## Context and value

`docs/comparison.md` named no competitor and let the positioning claim reach v0.11.1 unchecked. The
positioning epic (#444) requires a public field scan that names projects, says what to use instead of
this for most people, and lists this repository's own gaps before anyone else's. The scan is also the
evidence base for #230's amendment (#457) and the copy rewrite (#458).
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/459]
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/pull/467]

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** `docs/field-scan.md`, **when** a reader checks a comparative claim, **then** it cites a
   file path or URL, synthesised from the logged research run under
   `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/research/`.
2. **Given** the scan, **when** a reader counts, **then** it names at least ten projects and credits
   other projects by name.
3. **Given** the uniqueness claim, **when** it is stated, **then** it uses the bounded sentence and
   credits Burnd as the nearest neighbour.
4. **Given** the scan, **when** a reader opens it, **then** it lists this repository's own gaps before
   anyone else's and says what to use instead for most people.
5. **Given** `docs/comparison.md`, **when** a reader opens it, **then** it is a pointer to the scan, and
   `tests/test_doc_links.py` passes.

## Design

### Approach

Commit the research run (digests, source appendix, memlog and a staleness map with an earliest
re-check of 2026-12-01) under the research directory, and synthesise `docs/field-scan.md` from it. The
old comparison page becomes a pointer that keeps its one good line.
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/pull/467]
[Source: docs/field-scan.md]

### Decisions and alternatives

- **Commit the evidence with the page:** a fresh-context citation audit's corrections were applied and
  logged.
- **Keep third-party material out:** the four copied list READMEs are gitignored, and third-party
  contact addresses quoted from READMEs are redacted.
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/pull/467]

### Do not implement

Not recorded in the issue or pull request.
