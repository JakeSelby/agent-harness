## Goal

Bring always-loaded context (`CLAUDE.md`, the rules and the selected stances) to 200 lines or fewer, and keep it there with a lint check. The issue measured about 596 lines loaded on every turn (`CLAUDE.md` 51, rules 359, default stance selection 173) against Anthropic's guidance of under 200 lines for `CLAUDE.md`-class content. Much of it was rationale and procedure, which the repository's own placement ladder assigns to skills; the issue notes the cost is paid every turn even when cached, and competes with the task for attention.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** the rules, **when** they are loaded, **then** each keeps its operative lines and a one-line pointer to the skill that carries the rationale and procedure.
2. **Given** the stance variants, **when** they are loaded, **then** each is reduced to its preference statement, with the evidence moved to the matching skill or to `docs/`.
3. **Given** `CLAUDE.md`, the rules and the worst-case stance per dimension, **when** their lines are counted, **then** the total is at or under 200.
4. **Given** a tree whose always-loaded context exceeds the cap, **when** `bin/harness lint` runs, **then** it fails with a `context-cap` finding and a per-group breakdown naming the offender.
5. **Given** the cap, **when** it is read, **then** it is a constant in `bin/harness`.

## Tasks

- [x] Cut each rule to its operative lines plus a skill pointer (AC: 1)
- [x] Move rationale, examples and evidence verbatim into the owning skill, adding `transcript-hygiene` and `api-verification`, or into `docs/how-it-works.md` and `docs/preferences.md` (AC: 1, 2)
- [x] Shrink every stance variant to its preference statement (AC: 2)
- [x] Add `ALWAYS_LOADED_CAP = 200` and `check_context_cap` to `bin/harness lint` (AC: 3, 4, 5)

- [x] Update `CHANGELOG.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md` and `README.md`

## Dev notes

### Approach

The pull request records the result: always-loaded context, counted as `CLAUDE.md` plus the rules plus the longest variant of each stance dimension, fell from 583 to 187 lines. The issue's 596 was measured against the default selection; the pull request counts the worst case per dimension, so the two starting figures differ. Content moved verbatim, so no rationale was lost, only relocated.

### Bound decisions and requirements

- FR-38: Secret, personal-data and context-budget lint. That requirement now also carries a 4,202-token cap, which was added later (#474); this story delivered the 200-line cap.
- FR-55: Static context budget in CI is related but separate, and is not bound here.
