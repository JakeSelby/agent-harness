## Story

As the maintainer and as a user deciding whether to trust v1.0.0, I want a context-independent go/no-go
audit of the exact candidate artifact, retained in public, so that the release is signed off by someone
who did not build it and the verdict is tied to the bytes that ship.

## Context and value

Step 4 of the v1.0.0 release program, under epic #206. The plan starts it only when #207, #208 and #209 are
complete and the candidate is frozen, and #211 starts only after it records a go verdict.
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/210]
[Source: _bmad-output/planning-artifacts/v1-release-plan.md#Sequencing and ownership]

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** the v1 stories, the original #94 to #100 acceptance conditions and the lifecycle baseline,
   **when** the audit runs, **then** each is reconciled.
2. **Given** the exact candidate archive and digest, **when** it is audited, **then** package contents,
   checksums, a secret scan, dependency vulnerabilities and licences, third-party notices, workflow
   permissions and provenance are checked.
3. **Given** the README, install, support, SemVer, migration, rollback, troubleshooting and accessibility
   paths, **when** the auditor follows them as a first-time developer, **then** each is assessed.
4. **Given** compatibility evidence and public claims, **when** they are audited, **then** they refer to the
   candidate record.
5. **Given** a blocking finding, **when** it is recorded, **then** it has an owner, and the auditor rechecks
   the fix and never self-certifies implementation.
6. **Given** any change to the candidate commit or archive digest, **when** it happens, **then** the whole
   verdict is invalidated and the audit reruns in full.

## Design

### Approach

The reviewer is a human, or a model of a different family that did not implement the candidate. Reviewer
identity, inputs, findings, dispositions, reruns and the signed verdict are retained in a public audit
artifact. [Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/210]

### Decisions and alternatives

- **Independence by family or person,** not by a fresh session of the implementing model.
- **Any candidate change voids the verdict;** there is no partial re-audit.

### Do not implement

- Fixing findings inside the audit; fixes have owners and the auditor rechecks them.
