## Reproduction

The `plan-authoring` skill, its template and example, and the `planner` role all defaulted the Review
Card's `## System design` diagram to a mermaid `flowchart LR`. Open a card in the plan-mode pane or the
chat sidebar: the mermaid fence shows as raw source, so the card's one diagram is unreadable exactly
where the card is reviewed.
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/315]

## Root cause

The authoring guidance chose a diagram format that the review surfaces do not render. The plan-mode
pane and the chat sidebar show a mermaid fence as source; GitHub renders it.
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/315]
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/pull/316]

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** the template and example cards, **when** they are read, **then** the card diagram is a
   plain-text drawing in a `text` fence and neither carries a `mermaid` fence above the `---`.
2. **Given** a card diagram, **when** it is drawn, **then** it has at most 12 nodes, marks new or
   changed nodes with a `*` prefix named in the caption, and keeps lines under 80 columns.
3. **Given** the addendum below the `---` or a doc read on GitHub, **when** a diagram is needed,
   **then** mermaid remains available.
4. **Given** the `planner` role and its generated `claude/` projection, **when** read, **then** they
   follow the same rule.

## Design

- **Approach:** change the rule in the skill, its template and example, and the `planner` role, and
  regenerate the projection.
- **Decision:** a `text` fence rather than mermaid for the card's single diagram; mermaid stays for
  the addendum and GitHub-rendered docs.
- **Must not change:** the addendum's freedom to use mermaid.
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/pull/316]
