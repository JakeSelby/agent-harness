## Story

As a developer who has chosen a communication-style stance, I want a bounded semantic check that a
short response follows the resolved style rules, so that directness, tone and presentation are checked
where structural checks cannot reach. Closed as not planned on 2026-09-21; this file records the
proposal as history.

## Context and value

Structural checks cannot tell whether a short response follows the user-selected communication style,
and broader correctness judgments need context outside this use case. The original epic made this,
with skill selection, the first release. The epic's 2026-09-21 re-scope removed it: the first release
had checked the manner of the agent's prose, but nothing consumed those answers and nothing could
score them, so the provider moved onto decisions with a consumer and an observable outcome. #372 now
carries the bounded on-task comparison this issue had taken over from #146.

[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/135]

## Acceptance criteria

As proposed, not delivered:
1. **Given** a question, **when** it is sent, **then** it carries a small explicit payload, clear
   criteria and an insufficient-context outcome, and no full transcript or codebase.
2. **Given** paired fixtures for opposing stance variants, **when** they are judged, **then** each
   produces the appropriate finding; a disabled preference produces no check, and explicit user
   overrides are resolved before inference.
3. **Given** exact syntax, required headings and counts, **when** they are checked, **then** they stay
   deterministic.
4. **Given** a scope check, **when** it compares a supplied action with explicit boundaries, **then** it
   does not grant permission, infer consent or certify safety.
5. **Given** a caveat check, **when** it runs, **then** it requires the known caveats as input and does
   not infer omitted limitations from the response alone.
6. **Given** any pack, **when** it is considered for enabling, **then** its findings stay advisory and
   its misses, false alarms and abstentions have been evaluated.

## Design

### Approach

Response-only semantic checks against resolved, explicit rules, with exact formatting and counts kept
in code. Follow-on packs would compare one action with a short explicit scope constraint, or one
response with supplied known caveats.

### Decisions and alternatives

- **Closed as not planned (2026-09-21):** prose-manner answers had no consumer and no observable label,
  so the epic re-scoped to hook decisions.
- **On-task comparison carried by #372,** which supersedes #146.

### Do not implement

Generic action safety, injection detection and exfiltration screening were excluded even in the
original proposal.
