## Story

As a developer whose repository has its own documentation conventions, I want documentation style and document-revision policy to be selectable, so that public docs, private engineering notes and immutable records are each governed appropriately.

## Context and value

Fixed rules enforce a concise reference style, ban internal links, and prohibit rewriting all dated artifacts; the issue says this conflates public documentation, private engineering notes and immutable records ([#122](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/122)). On `main`, `primitives/rules/conciseness.md` still excludes "internal ticket numbers" from shipped text, and `primitives/rules/working-style.md` still says "never rewrite a dated artifact or an applied migration".

The epic's 2026-09-21 assessment rated the story Not delivered, with no dimension ([#116 comment](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/116)). No documentation or document-history dimension exists under `primitives/stances/` today.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** a selected documentation variant, **when** comment, doc and pull-request guidance and the authoring skill's examples are rendered, **then** they agree with that variant.
2. **Given** `document-history=living`, **when** a living document is revised, **then** it keeps version history, and dated evidence and applied migrations remain protected.
3. **Given** each alternative, **when** the rendered instructions are tested, **then** audience guidance leaks no secret and requires no link the audience cannot reach.

## Design

### Approach

The issue proposes two dimensions: `documentation` with `concise-reference`, `explanatory` and `repo-native`, and `document-history` with `append-only` and `living`. The defaults stay `concise-reference` and `append-only`. Applied migrations and audit evidence stay immutable whatever the style. A global ban on internal references is replaced by accessible, audience-appropriate links ([#122](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/122)).

The plan adds that public-facing document requirements are separated from private engineering references, and that repository pull-request templates are respected rather than one universal amount of prose imposed [Source: docs/plans/stance-completeness.md#Step 3 — Existing opinions].

### Decisions and alternatives

- Immutability of applied migrations and audit evidence is held independently of the style choice ([#122](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/122)).
- No other alternative is recorded in the issue or pull request.

### Do not implement

- Do not make applied migrations or dated evidence editable under any variant.
- Do not sync an unmerged candidate into the live installation.
