## Goal

This repository moves its planning onto BMad's native ticketing, `bmad-preview-ticketing`, once upstream ships it, and keeps what `scripts/bmad_issue_sync.py` guarantees today: every managed issue mapped, drift found daily, and IDs that never collide across branches. Until then this epic holds the gate, and its issue records what upstream decides and when.

## Scope and requirement coverage

**In scope:**

- The upstream contribution: rerun-safe GitHub publishing (bmad-code-org/BMAD-METHOD#2956, fixed in bmad-code-org/BMAD-METHOD#2957) and the reconcile proposal (bmad-code-org/BMAD-METHOD#2958).
- Tracking the gate below, and planning the spike once gate conditions 1 to 4 hold.

**Out of scope until the gate opens:** the migration itself (converting the mapped issues with AH IDs kept as aliases, re-pointing the three checks, customising `write`, retiring the overlapping commands in `bmad_issue_sync.py`), and porting `bmad_issue_sync.py` upstream.

**Requirement coverage.** §4.14 of the 2026-09-23 PRD. Adoption must keep FR-9 (traceable work items): the `issue-ownership` check still fails a pull request whose delivery issue is unmapped, and ID allocation still refuses an ID any reachable branch or worktree holds, which the preview does not do yet. It must keep FR-64 (story files as design records); how our story files map onto the preview's ticket files is a question for the migration plan.

## Architecture slice

Why the switch waits:

1. **Upstream says it is not ready.** While ticketing is in preview, `bmad-create-epics-and-stories` with `bmad-sprint-planning` remains the supported route; its stories are not read by `bmad-sprint-planning`, do not appear in `sprint-status.yaml`, and the current `bmad-build` does not move their status. Hooks are not integrated yet.
2. **It is not released.** It installs only through `npx skills add ... --skill bmad-preview-ticketing` and `bmad setup`; npm `latest` is 6.12.0, and `docs/bmad.md` pins `bmad-method` at 6.12.0.
3. **It is mid-rewrite.** bmad-code-org/BMAD-METHOD#2944 is the first of four parts that move build, review and retrospective onto the ticket tree and remove the sprint-status route.
4. **Two guards are missing upstream.** Rerun-safe publishing and a tree-to-tracker reconcile; our `issue-ownership` check and the daily live audit rely on both.
5. **Layout and identity differ.** `check_issue_ownership.py`, the CI audit and the traceability workflow read `issue-map.json` and flat `AH-*.md` files; the preview uses folders, per-epic integer ids and `tracker_id` frontmatter.
6. **It fits our customisation.** Acceptance criteria can stay local by editing our copy of the `write` verb, since the store config is ours and survives skill updates, and `tracker_status` already outranks `status` for board state.

## Sequencing

1. The bug report bmad-code-org/BMAD-METHOD#2956 and its fix bmad-code-org/BMAD-METHOD#2957, filed 2026-09-24 with the reconcile proposal bmad-code-org/BMAD-METHOD#2958 and a second small bug, bmad-code-org/BMAD-METHOD#2959 (setup recolours existing labels).
2. The reconcile proposal, waiting on a maintainer's yes. A yes starts its own plan round: six drift classes (missing on the tracker, unknown to the tree, filed twice, open or closed disagreement, parent disagreement, blocked-by against `after`), one fixture each in `scripts/tests/test_tickets.py`, 400 lines at most, on `dev` after bmad-code-org/BMAD-METHOD#2944 merges.
3. Gate conditions 1 to 4, all upstream.
4. The spike (condition 5), then the migration plan.

## Exit criteria

The gate, all of:

1. `bmad-build` and sprint planning read the ticket tree (the four parts of bmad-code-org/BMAD-METHOD#2944 merged).
2. A tagged BMad release ships the ticketing skill through npm.
3. Rerun-safe publishing (bmad-code-org/BMAD-METHOD#2956) or an equivalent is in that release.
4. A reconcile check is in it, or `bmad_issue_sync.py audit --live` keeps running beside it.
5. A spike, planned once 1 to 4 hold, re-points the three checks and keeps AH IDs findable.

The epic closes when the migration lands, or when it is dropped with the reason recorded here.
