## Story

As a developer working with an agent, I want the harness to flag when the agent repeats an approach or
re-asks an already-answered question in different words, so that it reconsiders before spending more
turns. Closed as not planned on 2026-09-21; this file records the proposal as history.

## Context and value

Agents can repeat an approach or ask an answered question in new wording, and such local repetition can
be checked without claiming to understand overall progress. The original epic listed it as a follow-on.
The 2026-09-21 re-scope closed it with the other prose-level checks: the provider moved onto decisions
the hooks already make, which have a consumer and an observable outcome.
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/144]
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/135]

## Acceptance criteria

As proposed, not delivered:
1. **Given** fixtures, **when** they are judged, **then** repeated approaches are distinguished from
   meaningful changes, and repeated questions from legitimately changed scope.
2. **Given** retry counts, deadlines and budgets, **when** they are enforced, **then** they stay
   deterministic.
3. **Given** missing local context, **when** a pack runs, **then** it yields insufficient evidence
   rather than a stall or approval judgment.
4. **Given** any answer, **when** it is applied, **then** it never infers standing authorization,
   suppresses mandatory approval, treats silence as consent or overrides a stop.
5. **Given** a notice, **when** it is shown, **then** it is advisory, bounded and rate-limited, and its
   useful detections and false alarms are measured.

## Design

### Approach

Two separate packs: semantic repetition within a small recent-attempt window, and a proposed question
against a short relevant exchange. A hit is flagged to the main agent for reconsideration.

### Decisions and alternatives

- The proposal itself removed general blocker classification, whole-task stall judgments and decisions
  about whether permission is required.
- **Closed as not planned (2026-09-21)** in the epic re-scope.

### Do not implement

Whole-task progress judgments, permission decisions, or anything that overrides a stop.
