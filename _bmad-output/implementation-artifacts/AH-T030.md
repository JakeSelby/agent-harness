## Goal

Make the landing copy lead with what the harness is: the control plane under the Claude Code and
Codex a user already runs, holding one checkout of their own primitives, projecting them into each
agent, enforcing them with hooks and measuring what every session did and cost. The previous
headline, "Find out which of your agent rules actually fire.", was word for word the hero of a
separate measurement tool the harness uses, and it presented one loop as the whole product. The
spend capability's title also claimed savings that the 0.13.0 cost replay does not support: it
measured the harness at 1.28 times bare Claude Code per passed task. The maintainer approved the
replacement strings on 2026-09-24; this task places them exactly.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** `product.json`, **when** it is read, **then** `headline`, `hero.title`,
   `hero.subtitle`, `hero.proof`, `description` and `github_description` carry the approved copy,
   and no string in it contains an em dash.
2. **Given** the capability grid, **when** it is read, **then** the `spend-less` capability keeps
   its id, is titled "See and steer what your agents spend" and keeps its pitch and features, and
   "Rules you can measure, and prune" is fifth rather than first, the others in their prior order.
3. **Given** `README.md`, **when** it is read, **then** its H2 is the new headline, the paragraph
   under it is the approved one, the replaced detector paragraph appears verbatim under "Rules you
   can measure, and prune", and its capability sections follow the order and titles of
   `product.json`.
4. **Given** the detector-count test, **when** the gate runs, **then** it still checks every
   published sentence that states a detector count, without requiring the hero proof to state one.
5. **Given** the merge, **when** `scripts/sync_about.py --check` runs, **then** the GitHub About
   matches `product.json`.
