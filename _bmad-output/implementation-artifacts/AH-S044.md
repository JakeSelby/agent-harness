## Story

As a developer, I want a research-depth preference with explicit stopping criteria, so that I can ask for a quick check or an exhaustive, corroborated search and always learn how complete the coverage was.

## Context and value

The search count and the source-verification guidance cannot express quick checking versus broad, corroborated research ([#127](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/127)). The epic's 2026-09-21 assessment rated the story Not delivered: no `research/` dimension, and one fixed search budget ([#116 comment](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/116)). On `main` the budget is still fixed prose, "Web search is capped per session — 200 calls on Claude Code — and shared by every subagent" [Source: primitives/rules/research-and-verification.md], and no research dimension exists under `primitives/stances/`.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** any variant, **when** research is reported, **then** it keeps evidence attribution, uncertainty and truthful gaps, and `exhaustive` is bounded.
2. **Given** a selected depth, **when** the research workflow and the API-verification guidance run, **then** they follow it and do not treat unverified claims as facts.
3. **Given** budget exhaustion, unavailable primary sources or conflicting evidence, **when** tested, **then** each case is reported honestly, and selection and projection stay consistent.

## Design

### Approach

The issue proposes `research` with `quick-check`, `source-led` and `exhaustive`, default `source-led`, each defining source coverage, corroboration and stopping criteria. The variants draw on the shared numeric budgets of #123, and exhaustion reports incomplete coverage rather than a false complete result ([#127](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/127)). The plan adds that every variant retains attribution and uncertainty [Source: docs/plans/stance-completeness.md#Step 4 — New choices].

### Decisions and alternatives

- `source-led` is the default ([#127](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/127)).
- Budgets are shared with #123 rather than owned by this dimension ([#127](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/127)).

### Do not implement

- Do not report exhausted research as complete.
- Do not sync an unmerged candidate into the live installation.
