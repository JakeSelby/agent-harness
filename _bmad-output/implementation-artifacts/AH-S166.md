## Story

As a developer weighing the measured-rules claim, I want a worked example of the instrument catching
shipped features that did not work, so that I can see the loop pay off with figures I can reproduce.

## Context and value

#429 (a stance measured as never firing) and #324 (a hook measured as not moving its own metric) were
filed as bugs, but they are the two receipts that the measurement loop works. The positioning epic
(#444) asked for them to be written up as its worked example.
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/461]
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/pull/470]

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** `docs/caught-in-the-act.md`, **when** a reader reads it, **then** it tells both stories,
   the delegation stance that never fired across nineteen headless runs (#429) and the brief-guard hook
   whose metric did not move (#324), each with the figure, the command that reproduces it and what it
   took to notice.
2. **Given** the doc, **when** it ends, **then** it states what the instrument cannot yet show.
3. **Given** every figure in the doc, **when** it is checked, **then** it traces to the issue bodies or
   a quoted command.
4. **Given** the field scan and the launch narrative, **when** a reader opens them, **then** they link
   to the write-up.

**Recorded undelivered item.** Criterion 4 is the issue's own. At `origin/main`, `docs/usage.md` links
the write-up, but `docs/field-scan.md` does not, and the launch narrative does not exist yet (#215 is
open). [ASSUMPTION: #461 does not name #215; that #215 writes the launch narrative is inferred from its
acceptance] The issue was closed as completed.

## Design

### Approach

One new doc with three sections: "A stance measured as never firing", "A hook whose metric did not
move" and "What the instrument cannot show yet", plus a pointer from `docs/usage.md`.

[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/pull/470]

- **Limits stated:** the doc closes with what the instrument cannot show.
