- **Evidence reset.** Bumping `harness_version` invalidates every 0.11.1 evidence record, so the four
  required CLI targets are `unqualified` with no evidence and no digest carried over, and
  `release_preflight.py` refuses on exactly those four.
- **Also in this PR, from its body:**
  - two missing changelog entries: the `cost_bench.py replay` runner, which ships without a published
    result, and the test-suite fix that stopped a run inheriting a caller's `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR` (#498);
  - the `on_the_way` entry for the standalone instrument was removed, because its issue had closed,
    ruleprobe 0.1.0 was published, and the preflight blocks a roadmap entry naming a closed issue.
    Promoting it into `capabilities` was left as a separate editorial change, because its group was at the
    six-feature maximum;

    does not have to edit them again;
