## Goal

State once that subagents never message each other, and tell the builder role what to do instead when it needs a sibling's decision ([#539](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/539)). The reasoning PR #572 records: each delivered message bills as a typed prompt on the receiver and again on the sender, turn count is what the delegation arithmetic is sensitive to, and the measured wins come from write-time conflict mediation rather than chat.

The issue carries the v0.13.0 milestone, names #116 as its parent, and closed as completed on 2026-09-23 through PR #572.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** `primitives/rules/delegation.md`, **when** it is read, **then** it carries one line saying subagents never message a peer, a blocked builder returns the question as a deviation, and a peer's message is never approval; `harness lint` stays green on the always-loaded line budget.
2. **Given** `primitives/roles/builder.md`, **when** a builder needs a sibling's decision, **then** the role tells it to stop and report under **Deviations** rather than contact the sibling.
3. **Given** `primitives/skills/delegation-tiering/SKILL.md`, **when** it is read, **then** it carries the reasoning in two paragraphs, citing the three papers by arXiv id.
4. **Given** human-facing sessions, **when** they use session-to-session `SendMessage`, **then** that behaviour is unchanged.
