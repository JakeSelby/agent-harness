## Story

As a contributor or reader, I want the 0.13 adoption release plan and the roadmap arc committed in
the planning artifacts, so that the ordering rationale for the adoption work is public and dated.

release, and #212's 2026-09-21 amendment points at this plan for the ordering rationale. Committing it
