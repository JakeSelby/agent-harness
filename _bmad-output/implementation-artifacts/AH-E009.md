## Goal

The rule-measurement engine ships as its own package: the transcript readers, the shell tokenizer, the
detector registry and the hit-rate report. Someone with no agent-harness installed can run it against
their own transcripts and instruction file. agent-harness then consumes the package back as a vendored
dependency, without changing how any installed home behaves.

Completion is observable through the issue's "sixty-second test": a stranger with a CLAUDE.md and existing
transcripts learns which of their own rules fired, without writing Python first.

## Scope and requirement coverage

**In scope,** from the issue's Stories section:

- Carve the engine into its own repository, with its own corpus and readers.
- A declarative detector format, proved by re-expressing the generic detectors in it.
- Publish 0.1.0 and pass the sixty-second test on a machine with no agent-harness.
- Vendor the wheel back, so that `rule-detectors.py` becomes this repository's rule pack.
- A labelled corpus for detector validity.
- Document the standalone path, and list `usage --rules` in `harness --help`.

**Constraints,** from the issue's Principles:

- `check_detectors` keeps gating this repository's rules throughout.
- `bin/harness` and `usage-log.py` keep loading `rule-detectors.py` by file path.
- Publication gates on one real home upgrade, not only on the test suite.
- The public claim stays bounded, and the nearest neighbouring project is credited.
- What the sensor cannot yet show is recorded, not hidden.

**Requirement coverage.** `epics.md` lists this epic against FR-19, FR-21 and FR-22. Checked against the
2026-09-23 PRD:

- **FR-19 Detector or reason for every rule** (implemented; engine vendored in 0.12, #488): #454.
- **FR-21 Standalone instrument** (implemented, 0.12; `ruleprobe` 0.1.0 on PyPI): #451, #452, #453.
- **FR-22 Detector validity** (partial): #455 shipped the labelled corpus and its CI gate in the `ruleprobe`

  detectors under the floor are #602, under AH-E013.

FR-67's first part, declarative detectors in standalone `ruleprobe`, was also delivered by #452. The

## Architecture slice

- **AD-13 Detector logic lives in `ruleprobe`:** the harness binds rules to detectors and vendors a pinned
  `ruleprobe` wheel it never forks, and a `ruleprobe` release means a harness version bump. The spine marks
  declarative loading in the harness as planned.
- **AD-21 Dependencies:** third-party code enters only as a pinned wheel in `lib/vendor/`, loaded by path.
  At `origin/main` that is `lib/vendor/ruleprobe-0.1.0-py3-none-any.whl`.
