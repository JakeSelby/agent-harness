# AH-T026 — Compatibility step 8 says a resumed session is not rerouted, but a headless resume legitimately is

## Goal

Make step 8 of the qualification procedure in `docs/compatibility.md` describe what a headless
client can actually show about a session begun before the band workers were installed, so the
`cost-posture` case and the step it is assessed against agree. Round 4 of the 0.13.0 native
qualification was assessed as not qualified in part because they disagree: the step said such a
session "is not rerouted", while the case accepts a resumed session that "was rerouted to worker-a
only after the resumed process announced it". The maintainer chose to amend the step rather than
change the case, because a headless client runs each turn as its own process: the session can only
be continued by resuming it, and the resumed process loads its agents from disk and announces them,
so routing to the announced worker is the runtime working as designed.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** step 8, **when** it is read, **then** it asks that a session started before the
   workers were installed keeps them out of its session record and that its spawn still succeeds.
2. **Given** step 8, **when** it is read, **then** it says why a headless resume may route to an
   announced band worker, and that a reroute to a worker neither the session's record nor the
   runtime's announcement named remains a defect.
3. **Given** `resume_verdict` in `scripts/native_acceptance.py`, **when** it is compared with the
   amended step, **then** every verdict it returns or raises matches a sentence of the step, with no
   driver change.

## Tasks

- [x] Replace the step's "not rerouted" sentence with the observable one (AC 1, 2).
- [x] Check the amended text against `resume_verdict`'s three outcomes: a widened record fails, a
  spawn that did not run fails, a reroute to an unannounced worker fails (AC 3).
- [x] Add `changelog.d/733.changed.md`.
- [x] Point the step's pinned observables in `tests/test_compatibility_cost_posture.py` at the new sentence.
