## Goal

Freeze the v0.10.0 source identity and publish an honest candidate support contract before native
qualification begins. The freeze had to merge before qualification evidence was captured, so that the
evidence's source commit stayed stable under squash merging. Parent: #220.
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/224]

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** the candidate, **when** it merges, **then** one merged source commit owns every
   qualification-affecting byte.
2. **Given** the catalog, **when** it is read, **then** the four CLI rows are required and the three
   GUI rows are unqualified previews with `required_for_release: false`.
3. **Given** the migration metadata, **when** it is read, **then** it covers v0.9.0 to v0.10.0 sync,
   conflict review, rollback and uninstall.
4. **Given** viewer wording, **when** it appears, **then** it says preview and separately installed,
   and makes no native-click or distribution-clearance claim.
5. **Given** the catalog, README, docs, generated notes and tests, **when** they are compared, **then**
   they agree.
6. **Given** the candidate, **when** the gates run, **then** lint, unit tests, the BMad audit, the
   generation check and release-note generation pass.
