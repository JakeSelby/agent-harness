## Goal

Re-baseline the product brief and the PRD through the update intents of `bmad-product-brief` and
`bmad-prd`, so that both describe the product on `main`.

The issue records why. The brief still described the 0.9 series, and the 2026-09-19 PRD had no
requirements for most of what had shipped since:

- measured rules and detectors;
- the usage ledger and cost postures;
- tiered delegation and isolated role workers;
- the decision-provider contract;
- telemetry export and framework integration descriptors;
- external primitive import;
- the cost benchmarks, Remote Control hosts and the plugin channel.

The PR body puts the gap at four releases and about 180 commits.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Given the re-baselined PRD, when the PRD reviewer gate runs, then it reports no open critical or high
   finding.
2. Given the brief and the PRD, when they are compared with `product.json`, `CHANGELOG.md` and the
   milestone plan, then they agree.
3. Given the 2026-09-19 brief and PRD, when the new run folders are written, then the old documents are
   marked superseded and point to the new ones, and are not rewritten. (Scope.)
4. Given every as-built capability, when the PRD is read, then the capability maps to an FR or to a named
   non-goal. (Scope.)
5. Given a contradiction in the design record, when the PRD resolves it, then it takes the latest explicit
   decision and lists the resolution. (Scope; the issue asks for the list in the pull request, and the
   delivered list is in the addendum's §2 and the PRD memlog.)

**Evidence for AC 1, as recorded:**

- The committed adversarial review (`review-adversarial.md`) counts 0 critical, 7 high, 22 medium and
  20 low against the v0.12.0 tag and the code.
- The PRD memlog then lists the corrections applied: status lines re-derived from tag containment,
  consequences corrected to match the code, and added coverage.
- It also lists the follow-ups to file with the epics work.
- The PR body says every finding was either applied or recorded as a follow-up.

The record holds no second reviewer run after those corrections. So "no open high finding" rests on the
recorded dispositions, not on a clean re-run.
