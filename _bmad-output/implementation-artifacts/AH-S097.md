## Story

As the maintainer, I want to widen distribution only once first-run evidence and direct feedback show
the repository can support the attention, so that a launch does not outrun onboarding and support
capacity.

## Context and value

The adoption epic (#212) says to fix observed onboarding friction before scaling channels, and to
measure usable attempts, repeat use and actionable feedback rather than impressions. This story is the
last step of that sequence: it follows the proof (#213) and the feedback cohort (#214). The issue is
open and has no milestone.
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/215]
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/212]

**Recorded contradiction.** The issue asks for a launch narrative "centered on user control, shared
primitives, and inspectable output". The positioning epic #444, delivered after this issue was filed,
made measured rules the lead ("Find out which of your agent rules actually fire") with stances as the
frame, and the 2026-09-23 PRD §1.2 records that front door. The narrative criterion below should be
read against the current positioning; the issue text has not been changed.
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/444]
[Source: _bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-agent-harness-2026-09-23/prd.md#1.2 Positioning]

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** the cohort's blocking findings, **when** the launch is staged, **then** each is resolved or
   explicitly deferred.
2. **Given** the launch, **when** it is announced, **then** a concise narrative is published, following
   the positioning current at the time (see the contradiction above).
3. **Given** the channels, **when** the launch runs, **then** release notes, community posts and broader
   channels are sequenced so support capacity is not overwhelmed.
4. **Given** the launch, **when** it has run, **then** usable installs, repeat use, issue quality and
   contributions are tracked.
5. **Given** the tracked results, **when** the launch closes, **then** a decision on the next channel or
   onboarding improvement to invest in is recorded.
