## Goal

Make the repository's own copy match its code, as Part A of the 2026-09-22 enterprise-review intake
found it did not: the landing copy counted nineteen detectors where the registry holds seventeen, the
field scan and worked example overstated gaps that had since closed, and the always-loaded figures did
not name their scope. A positioning built on measured claims cannot carry stale ones.
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/516]
[Source: _bmad-output/planning-artifacts/roadmap-intake-enterprise-review-2026-09-22.md]

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** `README.md` and `product.json`, **when** a test derives the detector count from the rule
   pack, **then** the copy states seventeen (six generic from the vendored wheel, eleven
   repository-specific), and the test fails when the copy drifts.
2. **Given** `docs/field-scan.md` and `docs/caught-in-the-act.md`, **when** a reader reads them, **then**
   they no longer claim an empty conflict engine (four constraints ship), an unsourced context cap, or
   no labelled corpus; they say the wheel's corpus scores six of seventeen and that the other eleven
   and the CI floor are #522.
3. **Given** `benchmarks/oracles/hook_ids.py`, **when** it is read and tested, **then** `EXPECTED_MODULES`
   is the glob at the task's `parent_sha`.
4. **Given** `benchmarks/static.json` and the `harness lint` context line, **when** they print, **then**
   each names the scope it counts.
5. **Given** `tests/context_budget.py`, **when** it is read, **then** `LINE_BUDGET = LINE_CAP`: the token
   cap binds and the line cap is the secondary guard.
6. **Given** the change, **when** it lands, **then** it is docs plus tests, with no rule or stance text
   changed.
