## Goal

Make the documentation and the delegation skill describe the cost posture layer as it works: what a
cost variant controls and how to write your own, how an orchestrating agent bands unnamed work and
states budgets, what the usage feed reports, and the two places where a native agent's settings are
fixed at a moment rather than live: effort at sync, routing at session start. The skill an orchestrating
agent reads still carried a static table of three postures and said a bare spawn runs one tier below the
session, and the preference, usage and runtime-control pages predated sidecars (#248, under #247), band
workers (#252, under #251), budgets in briefs (#254, under #253), the usage feed (#256, under #255) and
session-scoped routing (#258, under #257).
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/259]

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** the delegation skill, **when** an orchestrating agent reads it, **then** it has operative
   guidance for delegating unnamed work and for budgets, and restates no shipped numbers.
2. **Given** the preferences, usage, runtime-controls, framework and how-it-works pages, **when** they
   are compared with the code, **then** they agree.
3. **Given** the `Unreleased` changelog section, **when** it is read, **then** it reads as one coherent
   account.
4. **Given** the three cost stance files, **when** they are loaded, **then** they mention what their
   table sets without growing the always-loaded context.
