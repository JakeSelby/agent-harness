## Story

As the maintainer, I want #230's positioning acceptance amended rather than replaced, so that the
record shows why measured rules now lead and stances support, without rewriting the original
decision.

## Context and value

The positioning epic (#444) keeps #230's category phrase and anti-claims but revises its signature
mechanism. The field scan (#459) found three other projects shipping switchable behavioural variants,
and three of this repository's nine stance axes bind to enforcement, so "switchable personal stances"
could no longer carry the headline. Amending #230 first gave the copy rewrite (#458) a recorded
acceptance to meet.
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/457]
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/444]

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** #230, **when** the amendment is made, **then** it is appended as a dated amendment section
   and the original prose is untouched.
2. **Given** the amendment, **when** a reader reads it, **then** it keeps `user-owned agent harness` as
   the category phrase and #230's anti-claims.
3. **Given** the amendment, **when** a reader reads it, **then** "switchable personal stances" is a
   supporting mechanism and measured rules are the signature one.
4. **Given** the amendment, **when** a reader reads it, **then** `model-provider-agnostic` leaves the
   headline in favour of naming Claude Code and Codex.
5. **Given** the amendment, **when** a reader looks for the reason, **then** it records the three
   projects with switchable variants and the three of nine axes that bind to enforcement.

## Design

### Approach

A comment on #230 headed "Amendment, 2026-09-21 (for #457)", posted 2026-09-22, lists what is kept
and the two revised lines, and points to #458 for the literal copy. No repository file changed.
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/230]
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/457]

### Decisions and alternatives

- **Amend, do not replace:** the epic's principle is to keep #230's category phrase and anti-claims and
  revise only its signature mechanism.
- **Name the runtimes instead of "model-provider-agnostic":** the amendment gives the reason as two
  runtimes against rulesync's fifty-two.
- **Signature mechanism:** a detector per rule file, lint that fails an unmeasured rule, and hit rate
  per rule by repository and by selected variant.
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/230]

### Do not implement

- The copy itself; that is #458. [Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/457]
