## Story

As a developer who uses the harness, I want rules, stances, skills, roles and workflows authored once in
one shared source with reproducible native projections, so that each runtime receives the same policy
without a second, runtime-specific copy of its meaning.

## Context and value

This is the shared-source delivery slice of #94, under epic #93. The issue was opened to record ownership
of the already open PR #101 and adds no release scope. Native installation and qualification were tracked
separately, under #95 and #98, and publication under #100.
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/163]

## Design

### Approach

Primitives move under `primitives/` (rules, stances, skills, roles, workflows, instructions). One loader,
`lib/harness_core/catalog.py`, reads them for the CLI, the lint and role workers. Runtime bindings live
in `adapters/claude-code/bindings.json` and `adapters/codex/bindings.json`, and `claude/` becomes a
projection of the shared source. Compatibility paths keep existing integrations working during the
migration. [Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/pull/101]

### Decisions and alternatives

- **Preserve the rules already on main:** the worktree, personal-writing-profile and delivery-issue
  rules were carried into the shared authority rather than rewritten.
- **No support claim in this slice:** native acceptance and publication stay gated by #98 and #100.
- Alternatives considered are not recorded in the issue or pull request.

### Do not implement

- Native installation and qualification (#95, #98).
- Publication (#100).
