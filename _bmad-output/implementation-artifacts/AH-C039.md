## Goal

Run the vendored `ruleprobe` wheel's labelled corpus in this repository's CI, and label the eleven
repository-specific detectors, so that every detector `usage --rules` reports has a known precision
and recall. Before this, the wheel's corpus scored six of seventeen detectors, the other eleven were
unscored, and the floor was not a CI gate here (#516 corrected the copy to say so). This is the Part A
item 4 residue of the 2026-09-22 enterprise-review intake.
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/522]
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/pull/601]

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** `.github/workflows/ci.yml`, **when** CI runs, **then** a `corpus` job runs
   `scripts/detector_corpus.py --floor 0.9` over the wheel's corpus plus a repository corpus, and fails
   under the floor, on a stale below-floor record, or on a detector with no labelled example.
2. **Given** the repository corpus, **when** it is read, **then** each repository-specific detector has
   at least five synthetic positives and five near-miss negatives, with no real transcript content.
3. **Given** `docs/field-scan.md` and `docs/caught-in-the-act.md`, **when** a reader reads them, **then**
   they state the scored count, derived from both corpora by the existing figure test, and the floor
   as a CI gate.

**Recorded divergences.**
- `research/search-over-cap` carries two positives and two negatives rather than five: one positive
  costs a 201-search transcript and the detector fires at most once per session.
- Two detectors measure precision 0.83 at recall 1.00 on this corpus,
  `secrets/git-add-secret-file` and `autonomy/denied-by-grade`. They are recorded in
  `known_below_floor` with their measured pair and floor rather than the floor being lowered, and
  tightening them is filed separately (#602, per the PRD).
- The PR leaves whether the `corpus` job becomes a required check to the ruleset.

## Dev notes

### Design

- **Stance gates removed for scoring:** the scorer passes no stances, and the four gated detectors
  would otherwise score zero recall; gating stays covered by the detector tests.
- **Synthetic transcripts** from `tests/fixtures/detector-corpus/build_sessions.py`, so no real content
  or personal path enters the corpus.
- The PR records precision 0.99 and recall 1.00 over both corpora, across 84 positives and 94
  negatives: agreement with the labels, not field accuracy.
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/pull/601]
