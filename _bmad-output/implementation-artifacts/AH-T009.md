## Goal

Promote the project's research into BMad research artifacts under
`_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/research/`, so that the re-baselined PRD and architecture spine (#618,
#619) can bind to cited evidence rather than to conversation history.

The issue records why this was needed then. The project had done a lot of research: measurement studies,
spikes, evaluations and a field scan. Only the field scan existed as a BMad research artifact, and none
of the research had reached the PRD or the spine. It is the first child of AH-E012, because the
re-baselined documents cite what it produces.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Given each new research artifact, when it is read, then it cites its sources and states its method and
   date.
2. Given `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/source-ledger.md`, when it is read, then it lists every new
   artifact.
3. Given the pull request's diff, when it is scanned, then no unpublishable or personal material appears
   in it.
4. Given the re-baseline epic tree, when the pull request merges, then #616 and #617 to #623 have
   reserved BMad IDs in `issue-map.json`. (Scope item "Reservations".)

**Scope items not found in the delivered artifacts.** The issue's scope also asks to:

- record market and user-voice research as open gaps;
- cover clone statistics and the lifecycle runner under measurement.

A search of the five 2026-09-23 artifacts and of `source-ledger.md` at `origin/main` finds no market or
user-voice gap record and no clone-statistics section. Clone statistics appear in the PRD instead, as
counter-metric SM-C1. The lifecycle runner appears only as a lifecycle-acceptance timing in the platform
artifact. These scope items were not acceptance criteria, and the issue closed as completed.
