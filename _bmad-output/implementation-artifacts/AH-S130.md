## Story

As the release maintainer, I want a release to cost one native qualification round, and that round
to be cheap, so that releases stop spending hours and millions of tokens on rework.

## Context and value

The issue records that 0.11.0 took four rounds and 0.11.1 lost a freeze within minutes, with one
round of the four required targets costing about 60 minutes and about 1M orchestrator tokens, plus
0.75M–1.3M client tokens per Codex target. It traces the cost to four places:
- **Rework:** `compatibility.evidence_errors` rejected evidence on any diff under the runtime source
  paths, with no per-case or per-target scoping, and a hung orchestrator lost a whole round because no
  partial records were kept.
- **Hand-driven cases:** only `cost-posture` was automated and the runner had no Codex row.
- **`bmad-workflow`:** 52–73% of a Codex target's spend.
- **Mechanics:** about 12 sequential PRs at about 3 minutes of CI each, plus changelog conflicts.
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/340]

## Acceptance criteria

The issue states a goal and children, not acceptance criteria. Taken from the goal and the closing
comment:
1. **Given** a release, **when** it is qualified, **then** it takes one native qualification round.
2. **Given** each child issue, **when** the umbrella closes, **then** the child has landed or been
   filed as a decision.
3. **Given** the seven open owner decisions the issue lists, **when** the umbrella closes, **then**
   each is decided, delivered or filed.

## Design

### Approach

An umbrella over child issues, in a recommended sequence: small engineering before the next release
(freeze, incremental writes, runner defects, sequential BMad layers, fragments, as much of the smoke
tier as fits); the Codex home, scripted cases, per-target scoping, lighter workers and cheaper
orchestration in the integration-contract work; and per-case scoping, `bmad-workflow`'s place and the
cadence carve-out only after an owner decision.

### Decisions and alternatives

The issue lists seven open owner decisions: per-case scoping; per-target adapter scoping;
`bmad-workflow` while the confinement defect is open; cadence and its carve-out; smoke-tier
authority; the evidence-producing tier; and the release branch. Outcomes, from the child records:
- Per-target scoping was built (#333); per-case scoping is open decision #582.
- `bmad-workflow` was replaced as a required case by a generic framework-layering probe (#349, per a
  2026-09-21 comment).
- Smoke-tier authority remains open: #334 shipped the tier as advisory.
- A cheaper executor with a strong assessor was built (#338).
- The release branch was adopted (#332).
- The cadence carve-out for corrective and security releases is not found in the record (see #320).
