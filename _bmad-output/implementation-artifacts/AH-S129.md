# AH-S129 — Write qualification evidence incrementally so a killed round costs one case

## Story

As the release maintainer, I want each qualification case's result written durably as the case
finishes, so that a killed round costs one case instead of the whole round.

## Context and value

From the release-cost analysis tracked in #340; the issue says it was "not in the brief's list, and
cheap". Round 2 of the 0.11.0 release hung, its workers were killed, and the round's work was lost
entire. The evidence schema already unions cases across several records per client and rejects any
linked `failed` or `unverified`, so a partial record is a valid input. The issue names no honesty
risk, since the union and the failure-blocking behaviour are unchanged.
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/339]

## Acceptance criteria

The issue has no acceptance section; these are taken from its mechanism and the PR's tests:
1. **Given** a round killed after two cases, **when** the log is read, **then** it holds two durable
   results.
2. **Given** a progress log, **when** `--from-progress` runs, **then** it rebuilds a record carrying
   only finished cases, without launching a client; an empty log builds no record.
3. **Given** a resumed round, **when** its record is built, **then** its cases are unioned with the
   surviving ones, and a rerun case supersedes its earlier result.
4. **Given** lines from another commit or a torn write, **when** the log is read, **then** they are
   ignored.
5. **Given** the default log location, **when** a round runs, **then** it sits outside the checkout.

## Design

### Approach

`scripts/native_acceptance.py` appends each case's result to a durable log as it finishes;
`--progress` names the log and `--from-progress` rebuilds a record from it.
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/pull/397]

### Decisions and alternatives

- The evidence union and the rejection of a linked `failed` or `unverified` are unchanged; a partial
  record was always a valid input.

### Do not implement

Any change to the evidence schema's union or failure blocking.
