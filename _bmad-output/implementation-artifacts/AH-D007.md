## Context

The question is whether to add embeddings and semantic retrieval over the session archive (#543) and serve it to agents. It was deferred on 2026-09-22 with a reopen criterion, and not dropped ([#546](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/546)).

The forces the issue records:
- **Measured results in the literature:** 11 of 12 solver and memory-system pairings failed to beat memory-off on a September 2026 coding benchmark (arXiv 2609.23570); episodic memory never beat baseline on SWE tasks, and the authors blamed retrieval noise (arXiv 2505.23422); 800K benign tokens prepended raised missed dangerous actions 2 to 30 times (arXiv 2605.12366).
- **Direction:** the harness is trimming a 12,607-token standing context (#430), and injected retrieval moves the other way until an arm measures it.
- **Coverage:** session logs are identifier-dense. Issue numbers, paths, symbols and error strings are exact tokens that FTS5 matches; paraphrase recall is the embeddings case, and it is the minority on this corpus.
- **Trust:** a retrieved excerpt is laundered content of the kind `delegation.md` refuses to act on, and past transcripts contain web tool output.
- **Duplication:** the issue names an existing semantic-memory store the archive should feed as a source rather than rival.

## Options

1. **Add semantic retrieval with the archive.** It would cover paraphrase recall. Against it, per the issue: the cited results show memory systems mostly failing to beat no memory, and it adds context and a trust surface.
2. **Hold it behind full-text search until a measured criterion is met.** FTS5 covers the identifier-dense majority of queries, and the question stays open with a written reopen test.
3. **Drop it.** Not taken: the issue says the question is deferred, "not dropped".

[Source: [#546](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/546)]

## Decision

Hold (option 2), as recorded on 2026-09-22. Reopen only when all of these hold ([#546](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/546)):
1. #543 has been live for four weeks.
2. A logged sample of `harness sessions search` queries shows FTS returning nothing useful, where a human found the answer by paraphrase, on 20% of queries or more.
3. A pull-based retrieval arm on the cost bench beats no retrieval on normalised cost or pass rate.
4. A written note explains why the existing semantic-memory store cannot serve the same query.

The issue stays open as the record of the hold.

## Consequences

- The 2026-09-23 spine lists semantic retrieval as deferred "until the #546 reopen criteria are met", and the PRD's non-goals include "No semantic retrieval over sessions until full-text search demonstrably misses (#546)" [Source: _bmad-output/planning-artifacts/architecture-spines/architecture-agent-harness-2026-09-23/ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#Deferred] [Source: _bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-agent-harness-2026-09-23/prd.md].
- FR-63 lists semantic retrieval as out of scope on the same terms, and requires every archive search to be logged with a usefulness signal so criterion 2 can be evaluated [Source: _bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-agent-harness-2026-09-23/prd.md#FR-63: Coordination for parallel agents].
- Gap recorded: neither #543 nor #544 carries that search logging in its acceptance criteria, so criterion 2 has no delivering story yet.
- #543 and #544 are built as full-text search and pull-only recall.
