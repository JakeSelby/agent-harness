## Story

As a new reader who arrives from the repositioned front door, I want the next four docs to tell the
same story, so that I do not meet the old claims one click after the new ones.

## Context and value

The front door changed in #468 to lead with measured rules. `docs/how-it-works.md`,
`docs/preferences.md`, `docs/stance-demo.md` and `docs/getting-started.md` are the docs a new reader
hits next, and they still led with the old claims. The 0.13 release plan lists the change for each.
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/462]
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/pull/476]
[Source: _bmad-output/planning-artifacts/v0.13-adoption-release-plan.md#Step 4 — Reposition the front door]

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** `docs/how-it-works.md`, **when** a reader opens it, **then** it opens on the measurement
   loop and has a five-step rule lifecycle: write, detector or opt-out reason, lint, `usage --rules` by
   repository and variant, prune.
2. **Given** `docs/preferences.md` and `docs/stance-demo.md`, **when** a reader opens them, **then**
   their opening states that three of nine stance axes bind to enforcement.
3. **Given** `docs/getting-started.md`, **when** a reader follows the quickstart, **then** it names
   Claude Code and Codex rather than "model-provider-agnostic" and ends on `harness usage --rules`.
4. **Given** the change, **when** `tests/test_docs.py` and `tests/test_doc_links.py` run, **then** they
   pass unmodified.

## Design

### Approach

Edit the four docs as the release plan lists: the rule lifecycle as the spine of how-it-works, the
enforcement boundary early in preferences and stance-demo, and the quickstart ending on the per-rule
report rather than on `sync`.
[Source: _bmad-output/planning-artifacts/v0.13-adoption-release-plan.md#Step 4 — Reposition the front door]
