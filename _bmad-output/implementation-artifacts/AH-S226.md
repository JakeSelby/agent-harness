## Story

As a reader deciding whether to trust this repository's comparison with its field, I want the field
scan to credit the projects that now measure rule compliance and to claim only what they do not
do, so that the scan stays the honest, dated comparison it promises to be.

## Context and value

`docs/field-scan.md` §6 said this was "the only project I know of that binds a detector to each
rule file, fails lint on an unmeasured rule, and reports per-rule hit rate by repository and by
preference variant". The scan was read on 2026-09-21 with a re-check due by 2026-12-01. A scan run
on 2026-09-23 for ruleprobe's own planning found two projects the original run did not cover,
claude-md-doctor and RuleReceipt, that bind a check to each rule and report from Claude Code
transcripts whether it was followed ([#710](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/710)).
The literal claim still held, but its plain reading no longer did. The page's own rule is that a
comparison names its date and credits the field, so the correction lands now rather than at the
re-check.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** `docs/field-scan.md` §4, **when** it is read, **then** claude-md-doctor and RuleReceipt
   are named with what they do and a read date of 2026-09-23.
2. **Given** §6, **when** it is read, **then** the measured-rules claim says that binding a check to
   each rule is no longer unique and names only what the two projects do not do.
3. **Given** the page header, **when** it is read, **then** it records the 2026-09-23 amendment.
4. **Given** the research run behind the scan, **when** it is read, **then** both sources appear as
   a dated amendment with one digest each, and the 2026-09-21 findings are unchanged.
5. **Given** any other copy in the repository, **when** it is searched, **then** none repeats the
   old "only project" sentence.

## Design

### Approach

Amend in place with a dated note in the header, add two §4 entries and one sentence to §5, and
narrow the §6 claim. The research run is a dated artifact, so the sources are appended as an
amendment with new digests rather than rewriting its findings.

### Decisions and alternatives

- **Narrow rather than drop the claim.** The lint gate, the labelled corpus with a CI floor,
  per-variant grouping and the second runtime were each checked against both projects' READMEs and
  are still not found elsewhere.
- **Describe claude-md-doctor from its own source.** Its skill has the model write the matchers at
  each checkup and sample-verify fires; the entry says that and makes no claim that two checkups
  differ.
- **No `product.json` change.** A search found the "only project" sentence only in the field scan;
  the landing copy never repeated it.

### Do not implement

- The full re-check due by 2026-12-01.
- Changes to the landing copy or README.
