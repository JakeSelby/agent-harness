## Goal

Make every workflow preference effective across all policy layers, while truthfulness, authorization,
secret protection and native restrictions stay invariants that no selection can switch off. The issue's
Scope calls this the switch-completeness audit. The epic's later children add coordination and session
archiving for parallel agents. Completion is observable when each child's implementation and acceptance
tests have landed, as the issue's Completion section requires.

build-versus-buy, change scope, research, observability and gates. The children filed later add stance
constraints, the architecture-viewer contract, the subagent-messaging rule, and the coordination and

## Architecture slice

- **AD-2 One resolver, fixed invariants:** `posture.py` is the only resolver, and invariants sit outside
  every switch. This is the epic's outcome stated as a rule.
- **AD-6 One effective policy for every consumer:** every consumer asks `posture.py` for the effective
  policy. The spine's audit, which found a variant mismatch in a hook, the output style and telemetry,
  is the #117 problem.
- **AD-9 Checks bind subagents; decisions flow up:** the rule #539 states once.
- **AD-10 Integrations are descriptors bound at the hook port:** the architecture viewer from #199
  predates the descriptor contract and moves to a descriptor when its mailbox adapter lands.
- **AD-16 Parallel writers coordinate through shared, recorded state:** planned for v0.15.0 through #542
  and #543. Paths are declared, overlaps are logged, there is no peer messaging and recall is pull-only.
