## Story

As the maintainer deciding whether semantic retrieval should ever be added, I want every search of the
session archive logged with a signal of whether it was useful, so that #546's reopen criteria can be
computed from recorded rows rather than argued.

## Context and value

FR-63 requires that "Every archive search is logged with a usefulness signal, so the #546 reopen
criteria can be evaluated"
[Source: _bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-agent-harness-2026-09-23/prd.md#FR-63: Coordination for parallel agents].
No open story's acceptance criteria carried that signal, so #546's second reopen criterion had no
delivering story ([#693](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/693)). AH-D007 records the gap:
"neither #543 nor #544 carries that search logging in its acceptance criteria"
[Source: _bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/AH-D007.md#Consequences], and AH-S191 is the archive
story it would extend [Source: _bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/AH-S191.md].

#546's second criterion reads: "A logged sample of `harness sessions search` queries shows FTS
returning nothing useful, where a human found the answer by paraphrase, on 20% of queries or more"
[Source: _bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/AH-D007.md#Decision].

**What exists at HEAD.** The archive does not exist: `bin/harness` has no `sessions` command, and #543 is
open on v0.15.0 behind the #541 spike. The decision log does exist. It is local, append-only, never
model-visible, and it joins a later `outcome` row to a `decision` row by `decision_id`
[Source: policy/hooks/decisions.py]. This story is on the v0.15.0 milestone.

## Acceptance criteria

From the issue's Acceptance section ([#693](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/693)):

1. **Given** the session archive, **when** it is searched, **then** exactly one decision-log row is
   written for that search, carrying the usefulness signal.
2. **Given** the logged rows, **when** #546's reopen criteria are evaluated, **then** they can be
   computed from those rows.

## Design

### Approach

From the issue's scope: log every search of the archive to the decision log with a usefulness signal.
The signal records whether the result was used, or whether the answer was found another way. It stays
local and non-authoritative, as the archive is.
[ASSUMPTION: the search becomes a new decision point in `POINTS`, and the usefulness signal is written
as a later `outcome` row joined by `decision_id`, the pattern `grade-bash` already uses for `ran` and
`not_run`.]

### Decisions and alternatives

None recorded. Open for the builder:
- **How "found another way" is observed.** #546's criterion needs searches where "a human found the
  answer by paraphrase". The issue does not say how that is recorded.
  [ASSUMPTION: it needs an explicit mark from the developer or agent; nothing in the ledgers observes
  it today.]
- **Whether the row stores the query text.** The decision log caps stored input at 2 KiB and holds
  commands and briefs [Source: policy/hooks/decisions.py]. Whether a query may be stored is bound up
  with the body-storage question in AH-D009.

### Do not implement

- Semantic retrieval or embeddings; FR-63 keeps them out of scope until #546's criteria are met.
- Any export of the signal beyond what the decision log already allows; the signal stays local
  ([#693](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/693)).
