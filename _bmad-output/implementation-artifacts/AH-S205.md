## Story

As a developer running the harness, I want the output style and the listed skill and agent descriptions
trimmed to what they need, so that the always-loaded context is smaller without losing any rule or
trigger phrase.

## Context and value

#430 names three context-reduction changes and says each lands separately with a history row. This
issue lands changes 2 and 3: trim the `scannable` output style to its rules, dropping the worked examples
and the register table, and shorten the harness's skill and agent descriptions without losing a trigger
phrase. Change 1, deferring action-gated rule text behind hooks, is an architecture change with its own
spike, and the live prefix figure needs a paired bare-arm run; neither is part of this delivery, and #430
stays open for them.
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/588]

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** the trimmed style and descriptions, **when** the static estimate runs, **then** the
   always-loaded figure drops, and before and after rows are recorded in `docs/benchmarks.md`.
2. **Given** every primitive's trigger phrases before the change, **when** the test runs, **then** each is
   still present after it.
3. **Given** change 1 of #430, **when** its spike record is read, **then** it states the question, the
   experiment and a numeric exit criterion.

## Design

### Approach

- The `scannable` style keeps all nine of its rules; the worked examples and the register table go
  (1,528 estimated tokens become 862).
- Fifteen skill and eleven agent descriptions lose their capability restatements and keep every condition
  and literal user phrasing that triggers them (listings drop from 2,326 to 1,980 estimated tokens).
- The static always-loaded total drops from 7,524 to 6,519 estimated tokens.
- Change 1 is written up as an unrun spike in `docs/spikes/2026-09-22-deferred-rule-text.md`; nothing is
  implemented for it.

### Decisions and alternatives

- **Stop short of #430's targets.** The PR states the shortfall plainly: the style is 667 tokens lighter
  rather than 900, the descriptions 346 rather than 750. The rest is trigger text or a rule, and pushing
  further loses one.
- **History rows as a table.** `benchmarks/history.jsonl` cannot take a static row, because its schema is
  replay ratios, so the before and after rows are a table in `docs/benchmarks.md`.

### Do not implement

Change 1 of #430 (deferring action-gated rule text behind hooks), and the live prefix measurement.
