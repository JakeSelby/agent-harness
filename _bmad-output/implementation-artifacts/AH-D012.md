# AH-D012 — Record the layered, configurable, measurable harness vision in the brief and PRD

## Context

The evaluation and telemetry milestone needs a product model to plan against. The harness was described as a control plane under coding agents, but the brief and the PRD did not say which layers it has, what "switchable" means, or how each layer is compared. Writing that model down also collided with three PRD non-goals: "Not a model router", "Never caps spend" and "No trimming of a layered library".

## Options

- **Leave the vision implicit in the milestone plan.** Cheapest, but the plan would carry a product definition the brief and the PRD do not, and the three conflicts would stay open.
- **Rewrite the Vision sections of the brief and the PRD.** Reads cleanest, but it rewrites dated artifacts the repository preserves.
- **Append a dated amendment to both, with pointers.** Keeps the history intact, and a reader sees the amendment beside the original text.

## Decision

Append a dated amendment to the brief's Vision section and to PRD §1, with pointers from PRD §1.2 and from the spend-cap lines in PRD §8 and the brief's Scope. The three conflicts are resolved as follows:

- **Spend:** never denying for cost stays the default, and a spend cap is an opt-in module that denies only when the user switches it on.
- **Routing:** orchestration chooses a model class and effort for each role inside the host, and never selects a provider endpoint or serves a model.
- **Other libraries:** the harness cedes a whole area to another tool rather than trimming inside it.

The layer model is confirmed:

- six layers over the host baseline: observation, instruction, capability, enforcement, orchestration and advisory
- economy as a concern that runs across the layers
- a control plane that composes them, and an evaluation plane that measures them

## Consequences

- **Easier:** the milestone's epics cite one product model, and the four comparisons become requirements to plan. They are harness against bare, a unit in a minimal profile with and without the economy concern, a layer against an external equivalent in the same slot, and permutations of switches.
- **Harder:** every module must declare its claims, its surface and its instruments, and a module without an instrument is reported as unmeasured. The opt-in cap module and the slot adapters are new scope.
- **Must follow:**
  - PRD requirements and success metrics for the evaluation plane
  - architecture invariants for zero-context observation and estimand labels
  - the course correction that rewrites v0.14.0
