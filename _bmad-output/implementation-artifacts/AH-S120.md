## Story

As an orchestrator relying on soft budgets, I wanted a running subagent to hear about its budget while
it works, not only in its brief, so that an overrun could be corrected before the run ends. The
issue required the decision to be taken from telemetry first.

## Context and value

Deferred from 0.11. A subagent learns its soft budget in the brief and can count its own tool calls,
but hears nothing while it works. An in-run nudge needs an incremental transcript reader on tool calls,
the heaviest piece of the feed, so the issue asked to decide from `harness usage --by role` data
whether it earns its weight before building it.
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/322]

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** the ledger, **when** the decision is taken, **then** the decision and the numbers behind
   it are recorded on the issue before any code.
2. **Given** a decision to build, **when** the nudge ships, **then** it fires once per subagent at the
   posture's first `nudge_at` multiple, naming actual against budget, and denies nothing.
3. **Given** a built nudge, **when** it runs, **then** it never repeats for the same subagent, and
   `turn_feed: off` still writes nothing.

Criteria 2 and 3 applied only on a decision to build; the recorded decision was not to build.

## Design

### Approach

A spike over the local usage ledger, recorded as `docs/spikes/2026-09-22-in-run-budget-nudge.md`.

### Decisions and alternatives

- **Verdict, as recorded:** "do not build".
- **Numbers, from the PR:** 3,477 ledger rows; of 89 subagent runs whose brief carried a budget,
  2 overran (both builders), and the excess was 4.5 percent of subagent output, against a 10 percent
  exit criterion on both counts. The issue comment gives 2.2 percent of budgeted runs.
- **Deciding finding:** at the budget crossing the median run has one tool call left, and 23 of 51
  reconstructed overruns had none, so a `PostToolUse` nudge lands after the agent's last call.
- **Mechanism exists:** hook feedback does reach a running subagent's transcript, and the payload
  carries `agent_id`.
- **Reopen when:** over at least 200 budgeted rows, overruns and excess both reach 10 percent and the
  median run has ten or more tool calls left at the crossing.
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/pull/579]

### Do not implement

The in-run nudge itself, unless the reopen condition is met.
