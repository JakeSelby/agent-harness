## Reproduction

The issue records four rough edges in the usage feed, from a downstream soak of 0.11.0 and from the
0.11.0 release session:
1. `usage-feed: unknown finished, spend unknown` kept printing turn after turn. In the soak it began
   after two spawns were refused early; it also printed on a checkout that already carried #275, with
   no refused spawn, after background agents were stopped or resumed, so #275 did not cover every path.
2. An agent resumed with a follow-up message finished a second and third round with no budget line;
   only its first completion printed one.
3. For one builder the feed said 31,121 output tokens while the task notification said
   `subagent_tokens 102398`; the line did not say which it measures.
4. Nothing surfaced mid-run: a 36-minute builder was invisible until it returned, and a running
   `harness role run` worker published no token figure until it exited (one qualification case overran
   a 450,000-token cap to 985,530 this way).
The issue also records, as working as designed, that an overrun was reported (`over budget 1.1×`) and
the agent still finished with a complete report.
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/issues/306]

## Root cause

Not recorded in the issue or pull request beyond the symptoms. The PR's fix implies the feed kept no
record of having said the unknown line, tracked only one completion per agent, and never stated its
measure. [ASSUMPTION: inferred from the state keys the fix adds.]
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/pull/495]

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** an agent whose spend is unknown, **when** the feed reports it, **then** the line names
   the agent and is said once.
2. **Given** an agent resumed with a follow-up message, **when** each journalled stop lands, **then**
   the feed prints a cumulative round line, without counting a second agent.
3. **Given** the feed's first figure, **when** it prints, **then** a one-off line states what the feed
   measures, and a compaction owes that line again.
4. Mid-run spend (the fourth bullet) is out of scope here; it was split into #493.

## Design

- **Approach:** `policy/hooks/usage-feed.py` gains three pieces of state: `said_unknown` (agents
  already reported as unknown), per-agent `rounds` and `said_measure`. The new state keys are
  validated, bounded and preserved across a transcript rotation. `docs/usage.md` gains three
  paragraphs in the feed section.
- **Split:** surfacing a running agent's spend mid-run moved to #493.
- **Must not change:** the feed is already listed as a capability; this corrects wording and coverage
  and adds no capability (the PR's landing-copy line).
[Source: https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness/pull/495]
